How to record an online call
  1. Wear headphones so the call doesn't echo into your mic.
  2. Press Start recording and allow the microphone.
  3. A share window opens. Windows: pick "Entire screen" and turn on "Also share system audio" (works with the Zoom or Teams app). Mac: join in a Chrome tab (Meet, or Zoom/Teams on the web), pick that tab and turn on "Also share tab audio".
  4. Ask everyone to say their name once at the start ("Hi, this is Priya").
  5. Press Stop when the meeting ends.
